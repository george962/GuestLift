What TableSignal can see in your Square account

You connect through Square's own approval screen. Access is read-only, you can see exactly what we asked for, and you can turn it off yourself at any time.

What we read

  • Your business name and locations
  • Orders: time, location, totals, discounts, tax and tips
  • Payments and refunds, to get revenue right
  • Square's anonymous guest ID on an order, to count return visits

What we never get or keep

  • Card numbers or bank details
  • Guest names, emails or phone numbers
  • Any ability to change your menu, prices, payments or staff
  • Any ability to message your customers

The exact Square permissions we request

MERCHANT_PROFILE_READ · ORDERS_READ · PAYMENTS_READ

All three are read-only. Square shows this list on its approval screen before you agree.
