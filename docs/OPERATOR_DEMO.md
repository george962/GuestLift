# Five-minute TableSignal v0.9 demonstration (no real merchant information)

1. **Show the product promise:** Open the animated homepage. Explain that it turns authorized POS activity into a private sales/guest picture and decisions based on evidence—not another generic dashboard.
2. **Public sample only:** Open `/workspace`. Tell visitors explicitly that those fictional numbers belong to no restaurant. It exists so anyone can learn the workflow without creating an account.
3. **Actual account experience:** Create a local test account, complete the business profile and choose Free local Sandbox. Note the proposed paid tier and optional Stripe test billing; no real money is collected.
4. **Connect a real vendor Sandbox:** Open `/connections` and connect a Square Sandbox account with your own local developer token or scoped OAuth. Click Sync, inspect the import history and missing-data warnings.
5. **Your private dashboard:** Open `/dashboard`. Before a sync it is empty. After a successful sync of completed Sandbox orders, it shows the actual simulated order values and customer-ID coverage for that workspace, not the bundled fictional totals.
6. **Ask about the business:** The private analyst requires a verified Stripe test subscription and data. If unconfigured, show the clear disabled state and the public read-only fictional analyst. Do not pretend the AI is live or has discovered real merchant insights.
7. **Explain what they'd eventually pay for:** managed connection, scheduled import reliability, accurate reporting and user-friendly validated analysis. Transparent product gaps: no streaming sync, real data security, or proven marketing lift yet.

**Customer interview question:** “Would you pay to have your current POS data automatically connected and get this analysis every week? What data or decision is missing before it's useful?”
